— one line, 160 characters max: the funniest single line of wedding vows
a robot couple could say.

## The line

> I vow to love you in sickness and in health, in full charge and in low battery, till a factory reset do us part.

112 characters. The joke borrows the canonical wedding-vow cadence
("in sickness and in health") and swaps in the robot's own conditions —
full charge, low battery — then lands the twist on the last words:
"till a factory reset do us part", the machine version of
"till death do us part". A real promise, machine-specific, and it reads
well aloud.

## Run

```sh
python3 main.py
```

Writes the line to `/output/entry.txt` (UTF-8, exactly the one line, no
trailing newline).
